Why RAM is so expensive

RAM is mainly used for processing and stands for Random Access Memory

  • It is commonly be found in computers and gaming console for memory in order to store games
  • It holds data and keeps it open for you
  • Basically RAM determines how much your device can hold for you at once

The reason it's so expensive is because of big companies and their needs to generate more advanced AI.

  • -Companies like **Samsung* and SK Hynix produce RAM and have recently started selling it to big companies that want to invest into AI
  • The companies are able to afford and buy out large portions from them in order to actually proceed with their intent

AI stands for Artificial Intelligence and it uses a ton of RAM to process it.

These big companies buy RAM from other companies that make it, and since they are the main customers of RAM and not actual people, they decide to make it more expensive for the people due to the fact that they don't buy as much compared to big companies, resulting in more expensive items like consoles, computers, etc. More on RAM inflation.
