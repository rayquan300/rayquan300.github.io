Why RAM is so expensive

RAM is mainly used for processing and stands for Random Access Memory

It can commonly be found in computers and gaming console for memory in order to store games

The reason it's so expensive is because of big companies and their needs to generate more advanced AI.

AI stands for Artificial Intelligence and it uses a ton of RAM to process it.

These big companies buy RAM from other companies that make it, and since those are the main customers of their product and not actual people, they decide to make it more expensive for the people.
